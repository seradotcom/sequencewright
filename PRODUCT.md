# Sequencewright
An application-owned audiovisual workshop in the Semwright family, distinct from Launchwright. Editors and external agents direct, compare and revise scenes against shared immutable revisions. Audience: creators working at desktop displays for extended editing sessions. The editor should feel at home beside established professional video studios. Primary task: open a project, adjust composition and timing, review a proposed change, and preserve a reproducible candidate. English product UI; public AGPL-3.0-only source. No telemetry, cloud subscriptions or automatic publication. Application owns SQLite, revision CAS, branches and locks. Native SDK provides cooperation, not another Broker. Native renders, Graph and Effects must not be represented as verified until the real Host supplies evidence. Keep dark and light modes, keyboard access and reduced motion. Native builds and renders belong in remote CI. No deletion of user files. All design and product decisions are delegated by the owner.
